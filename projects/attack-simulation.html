01 / THE CHALLENGE

Problem & context

Understanding realistic attack methods can inform better assessment priorities, logging requirements, and remediation recommendations.

02 / THE APPROACH

How I approached it

01

Establish scope

Restricted activities to purpose-built lab hosts and guided scenarios.

02

Validate conditions

Explored vulnerable-host concepts and technical prerequisites for exploitation.

03

Observe the network

Examined packet captures and session-related behavior using network analysis tools.

04

Translate to defense

Connected observed behavior to patching, segmentation, access control, and monitoring considerations.

03 / OUTCOME

What it demonstrated

Developed a stronger connection between exploitation mechanics and practical defensive control recommendations.
