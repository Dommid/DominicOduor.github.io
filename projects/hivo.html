01 / CONTEXT & CONTRIBUTION

Client work, documented responsibly.

Engagement

As part of a six-person graduate cybersecurity capstone team, I supported an authorized assessment for Hivo. My responsibilities included leading web application penetration-testing activities and contributing a risk assessment framework and prioritization approach.

The assessment was organized using OWASP Web Security Testing Guide (WSTG) categories and documented through test records, security observations, and assessment reporting.

04 / RISK RATING METHOD

Make prioritization explainable.

The workbook used OWASP's likelihood-and-impact approach. The figures below are synthetic educational inputs, not Hivo scores or a representation of any client finding.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO — FICTIONAL

How the model works

Likelihood combines four threat-agent factors and four vulnerability factors. Impact combines four technical factors and four business factors. Each is averaged on the 0–9 OWASP scale before mapping the two dimensions to the severity matrix.
