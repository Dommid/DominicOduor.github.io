01 / THE CHALLENGE

Problem & context

Security analysts need to evaluate alerts, confirm what the evidence supports, and preserve clear investigation notes before recommending response.

02 / THE APPROACH

How I approached it

01

Collect evidence

Reviewed event and system-log information from lab systems, including authentication-related evidence.

02

Triage activity

Considered suspicious event patterns, sources, timing, and the difference between an alert and confirmed compromise.

03

Organize investigation

Used Wazuh and TheHive in guided exercises to connect monitoring and case-management workflows.

04

Document response

Practiced recording observations, assessing scope and preparing response or remediation steps.

03 / OUTCOME

What it demonstrated

Built practical familiarity with evidence-based triage, incident documentation, and the relationship between security monitoring and response workflows.
