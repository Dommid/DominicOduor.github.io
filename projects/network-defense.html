01 / THE CHALLENGE

Problem & context

Good security monitoring depends on a sound network baseline, controlled access, and knowing what normal or abnormal traffic looks like.

02 / THE APPROACH

How I approached it

01

Define boundaries

Worked with LAN/WAN interfaces and security zones in pfSense lab scenarios.

02

Enforce policy

Configured firewall rules and evaluated how allow/deny decisions affect reachability.

03

Secure connectivity

Practiced VPN server configuration and considered secure remote-access patterns.

04

Observe traffic

Used network logging and packet inspection to support understanding of control effectiveness and suspicious behavior.

03 / OUTCOME

What it demonstrated

Strengthened network-defense skills across routing, firewall policy, VPN fundamentals, and monitoring techniques.
